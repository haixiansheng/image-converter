Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our core commitment

Your images never leave your device. All processing (decoding, redrawing, re-encoding) happens locally inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or transmitted to any server.

2. What we collect

  • We do not collect the images you process
  • We do not collect names, email addresses or phone numbers
  • No registration or login is required
  • We set no tracking cookies of our own (third-party ads may set their own)

3. Third-party advertising

This site may display third-party ads (such as Google AdSense), which may use cookies to serve personalised ads and collect technical data such as IP address and browser type.

Manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out via aboutads.info.

4. Cookies

This site itself sets no cookies. Disabling cookies does not affect the tool's core functionality.

5. Logs and analytics

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may keep access logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We collect no additional logs ourselves.

6. About EXIF data

Conversion discards the original EXIF metadata (including GPS coordinates), which protects your location privacy.

7. Children's privacy

Intended for general audiences and not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

Updates will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.
