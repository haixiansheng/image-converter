FAQ

Q: Are my images uploaded to a server?
A: No. Everything runs in your browser. Images never leave your device, and it works offline.

Q: My PNG → JPEG transparency turned black elsewhere. What happened?
A: That tool didn't composite the alpha channel. This one detects transparency, tells you, and fills it with a colour you pick (white by default).

Q: Can I make a JPEG transparent by converting it to PNG?
A: No. JPEG stores no transparency data, so converting just changes the container — the opaque pixels stay opaque. Real cut-outs need image editing.

Q: Does converting lose quality?
A: Depends on the pair:
· To PNG, or WebP at quality 100 → practically lossless
· To JPEG → lossy, but above quality 92 it's visually imperceptible
· JPEG → PNG → won't improve anything; existing compression artefacts carry over

Q: Do you support HEIC (iPhone photos)?
A: It depends on your browser. Safari can decode HEIC; Chrome and Edge generally cannot. If the preview fails, your browser lacks the codec for that input format.

Q: Is EXIF data preserved?
A: No. Redrawing on Canvas drops EXIF metadata, which conveniently also removes location privacy data from your photos.
