About

A free, open-source, privacy-first online tool

Why we built this

Format conversion is a daily need: PNG screenshots that are too large, websites that want WebP, legacy systems that only accept JPG, HEIC photos other people can't open. But the usual online converters upload your image to their servers first — and the images people convert are often screenshots, IDs and internal documents.

So we built this: all conversion happens inside your browser. Your image never leaves your device. No sign-up, no fees, one image in, one image out.

How it works

  1. Decode — the browser natively decodes the source file (JPG / PNG / WebP / BMP / GIF, plus HEIC where supported).
  2. Redraw — the image is drawn to a Canvas. When converting to JPEG and the source has transparency, the background is filled with your chosen colour first, so you never get black blocks.
  3. Re-encode — encoded as the target format (PNG lossless / JPEG lossy / WebP high-efficiency).

No server-side computation is involved, so it even works offline.

About transparency

PNG and WebP support an alpha channel; JPEG does not. Converting PNG to JPEG therefore requires "compositing" transparent areas onto a background.

Many tools handle this poorly, producing black or noisy blocks. This tool detects transparency, tells you about it, and lets you choose the fill colour (white by default).

Privacy bonus

Redrawing on Canvas discards the original EXIF metadata (capture time, device, GPS location) — conversion that also protects your privacy.
